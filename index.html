Eric Yang

Modeling & Analytics Researcher at Moody's

I am a Modeling & Analytics Researcher at Moody's, where I develop quantitative frameworks for credit portfolio modeling and risk assessment. I hold a Ph.D. in Economics from George Washington University, an M.A. in Economics from Boston University, and a B.A. in Philosophy from Renmin University of China.

My academic work—spanning macroeconomics, monetary policy, and international finance—underpins my approach to designing data-driven models that enhance credit strategy and portfolio-wide decision making. I leverage advanced econometric techniques and industry data to deliver actionable insights on credit risk and capital allocation.
